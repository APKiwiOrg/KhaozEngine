# Renderer profile deltas

Format khaozengine.renderer-profiles/1. Deltas are candidate minus baseline within one unit. Separate series, no cross-backend deltas.

Times are ns. Percent is the relative mean delta. PerPass totals are diagnostic. Three iterations are within-job repeats, not independent fleet samples or statistical significance. Combined rows are descriptive. No regression budget is assigned.

The M5 colour copy runs outside any named pass, so its cost appears only in `gpu.frame`. Ownership is tracked in [#1636](https://github.com/APKiwiOrg/KhaozEngine/issues/1636).
