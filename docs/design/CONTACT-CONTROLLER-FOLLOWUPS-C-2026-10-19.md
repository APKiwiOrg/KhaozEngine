## Problem and evidence

The captured eight-hull stairs at a binary32 quarter-turn yaw contain a tread-edge query where
the real polynomial of the stored quaternion and Bepu's represented matrix give opposite exact
disc discriminant signs. Keeping both in one source box makes a uniform certificate impossible.
The shell sweeps are already Clear. Support alone makes six original zero-origin rows refuse.
A test-only represented-map projection made the unchanged original compound tick Advanced with
all six footprint requests Complete. That proves feasibility, not complete acceptance.

The radius-0.3 capsule at x `-0.7f` is a separate bounded work explosion. One Find processed
1,243 root cells and 3,301,460 instrumented events before the 1,024-cell frontier refused.
The graph peaked at only 147 nodes because each endpoint evaluation restored its checkpoint.
Adjacent binary32 queries were Walkable below 1,000 events. Root storage refusal surfaced only
as Unsupported. The cylinder outside-end sibling also exhausted a diagnostic count cap in surface
admission, so its interrupted count is not a completed-query total.

Research #1487 proposed a crown certificate as the primary repair under the old source box.
FU-R4 gated it after FU-R2. Task 5 selected and implemented the crown branch. FC-R10 retained
it and corrected the cylinder statuses from the independent oracle. Task 6 then repaired cylinder
admission cost with bit-identical public outputs. The measurements above remain historical evidence.
The report refutes an infinite loop for its exact input. It does not justify a timeout or a retry.

The existing SG-1 to SG-24, SC-9, SC-17, CF-1 to CF-7 and GS-1 to GS-16 remain binding except
for the explicitly derived support-source and work-policy dispositions here.

## Requirements

### CC-1. Capture one represented support authority

Support uses pinned BepuPhysics 2.4.0 `Matrix3x3.CreateFromQuaternion` on the actual installed
leaf quaternion. Capture the scalar and every broadcast `Matrix3x3Wide` lane and require matching
binary32 coefficient bits. Preserve stored signed zeros. The separate conjugate unit-axis
correspondence check remains numerical dyadic equality, so an equivalent opposite zero there
does not introduce a new refusal.

Each coefficient operation is binary32. With doubled components, products and left-associated
diagonal subtraction, the row-basis vectors are
`X=((1-yy)-zz,xy+zw,xz-yw)`, `Y=(xy-zw,(1-xx)-zz,yz+xw)` and
`Z=(xz+yw,yz-xw,(1-xx)-yy)`. Treat the resulting entries as exact dyadic inputs afterwards.
Do not replace this graph with binary64 formulas, Matrix4x4, Vector3.Transform or a normalized quaternion.

Compounds first call `Compound.GetWorldPose(child.LocalPose,parentPose,out leafPose)`, including
hull-centroid wrapper offsets, then capture that leaf's matrix and binary32 translation.
Do not multiply reconstructed parent/child matrices or reassociate position sums.

### CC-2. Separate support admission from retained legacy admission

Add `InstalledPoseOperator.TryCreateSupport(in RigidPose pose, out InstalledPoseOperator result)`
and `InstalledPoseOperations.ProveSupportComposition(in RigidPose child, in RigidPose root,
in RigidPose world)`. Factor coefficient capture and rounded composition proof into shared cores.
The support door must not call dual `TryCreate` first and extract its represented fields. That
would retain a dependency on the discarded box's determinant or inverse proof.

Retain finite/local/pose domains, the `1 +/- 2^-20` squared norm band, scalar/SIMD continuation
checks and positive determinant. Compute determinant, Gram and inverse transpose from the point
map itself. The represented map is slightly non-orthogonal. Mesh preimages use its actual inverse,
and normals use transformed edge crosses or inverse transpose, never an assumed rigid transpose.

### CC-3. Route every planar support door before deriving facts

Provide operator-accepting CaptureLeaf and TransformVertex/TransformVertices cores. Existing
feature wrappers keep their PosePoint correspondence check and dual factory. Support passes an
already proved support operator to the same topology and affine arithmetic implementation.
Preserve operand/Add order, local bounds, world bounds, winding, ordinals and exact-zero behavior.

Route both polyhedron capture overloads, cached local-topology materialization, both mesh doors,
triangle materialization, immutable world/mesh forms, fallback/reference capture and support-only
pose/composition guards. Rebuild vertices, edges, normals, domains, topology meetings and shared
member facts from this operator together. A post-validation SourceMap replacement is forbidden.
The support proposal pose-domain check must also leave PosePoint's alternative geometry behind.

### CC-4. Make planar sources point-valued without erasing arithmetic error

Use `SupportPolygonSourceMap(Vector3 X, Vector3 Y, Vector3 Z, Vector3 Translation)`.
BeginSource verifies exact represented X/Y/Z before extracting them. Never cast an interval
midpoint or an inexact FeatureNumber.Value. Compare/hash all twelve binary32 bit patterns for
source sharing and source-predicate reuse, including signed zeros. That sharing never merges raw rows.

Install the nine coefficients with `_work.Constant`, retaining component order, source-local
subtraction before mapping, exact translation/axis subtraction and expression lifecycle.
For an edge, certify `E=M*(b-a)`, `P=M*a+T-axis` and
`D=dotXZ(E,E)*r*r-crossXZ(P,E)^2` from original dyadic operands. Do not pre-round derived E, P or D
into a new authority. Closed-disc equality, denominator guards and radical root ordering remain.
Generic interval-polynomial tests may remain, but no production support map has box coefficients.

### CC-5. Give every curved consumer the same represented column

`SupportCurvedLeaf.SourceColumn` becomes Vector3 holding represented Y.
`SupportCurvedSourceArithmetic.Reset(Vector3 source)` installs exact constants.
Remove obsolete alternative-transform Source nodes, interval-column storage and compilation
branches when unused. Retain the bounded expression graph, exact scalar arithmetic, denominator
signs, radical elimination, degree/compile ceilings and all geometric parameter intervals.

Both curved capture overloads, normalized Axis/Across, neighborhood tangents, upright/horizontal
specializations, tilted extrema, whole-surface admission and lowest proposal contacts use that
column. The unit axis is its exact mathematical normalization with outward enclosures. Radius and
half length retain the circular primitive definition. Do not interpret the full matrix as an ellipsoid.

### CC-6. Bound cumulative curved work once per complete Find

Use one scratch-owned work owner shared by all curved arithmetic, root and surface-admission
kernels during a complete footprint request. A Find performs one such request. Direct footprint
callers receive the same independent ceiling. A memo hit does no certification work.

Charge one unit before each source `New`, `Sign`, recursive `Split` and recursive `Compile`
entry, each processed root cell, each root Refine iteration and each processed admission cell.
Count cache hits at those entry points too, matching the research proxy. This bounds repeated
predicate work that graph storage and per-sign compilation cannot bound. It is not a count of
every machine operation. Existing per-event node, expansion and polynomial ceilings still apply.

The ceiling is **1,048,576 accepted work events**, derived as 256 existing raw-member slots
times 4,096 existing source-graph node slots. This provisions a pooled graph-sized work allowance
per raw slot, not a new per-member limit and not a claim that node storage already bounds work.
The recorded certified ordinary Finds consumed 549 to 1,257 events, with nearby end rows at
647/978 and the standalone crown proof at 78. Those establish a functional scale below one
4,096-event allowance. The captured pathological 3,301,460-event query exceeds the pooled ceiling.
All represented-contract certified strata and maximum admitted raw-member fixtures must fit the
pool in both optimized and reference tests. If they do not, repair the proof/work cause and report
the failing row. Do not silently enlarge the ceiling. No time-derived multiplier is permitted.

Begin resets only at the complete request boundary. Leaf/part transitions, both proposals, the
below-band pass, source Reset, PolynomialSign, root solves and checkpoint Restore do not reset it.
Reject the next event before executing it once Consumed equals Limit. Exhaustion is sticky, even
if graph Restore makes ordinary arithmetic resolved again. Later sub-solves cannot publish success.
Nested/concurrent requests use independent scratch owners. Tests may inject only a smaller limit.
The enforcement runs with diagnostics disabled and allocates no object per Find after provisioning.

### CC-7. Publish exhaustion diagnostics and refuse atomically

Diagnostics report Consumed, Limit, each charged category, root/admission cell high water and
the first failure reason. Distinguish WorkLimit, RootStorage, TerminalRoots and AdmissionStorage.
Later restores, sub-solves or an outer Unsupported mapping cannot overwrite that first reason.
The category sum equals Consumed, which never exceeds Limit. Retain the public refusal mapping
unless another established contract requires otherwise. Visibility does not require a new public status.

Every failure remains a zero-written refusal with caller member/join spans and unused tails
untouched. No partial prefix, lower-floor fallback, dropped relevant raw member or exception-based
work abort is permitted. Storage-only root failures must be observable even when WorkLimit did not fire.

Cylinder statuses are independently derived from exact represented geometry, respectively Walkable,

without undoing the capsule crown selected under FU-R4.

Retain the capsule-wide global-crown certificate selected under FU-R4 before Side for the footprint role.
