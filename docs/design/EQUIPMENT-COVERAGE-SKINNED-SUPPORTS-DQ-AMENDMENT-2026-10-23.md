Status: owner approved on 2026-10-23. The owner approved bounded skin dips around straps, four body regions, up to eight covering sources, investigation of 0.5 mm leather clearance, and retention of the existing stance and stack gaps. Final collar and recession appearance limits are selected from renders before content admission. The owner explicitly retained 32 actors and the 15 percent aggregate GPU overhead gate. Added CPU preparation remains at most 2 ms at 32 actors, with zero warm managed allocations. This approval does not accept a measured cost miss or authorize a timing run.

This is the approved architectural amendment. Its [implementation plan](../superpowers/plans/2026-10-23-coverage-skinned-supports-dq.md) requires concrete-plan review before runtime implementation, using the existing Codex-only SDD method with fresh task review. Runs, releases and private adoption keep their separate gates.

This amends [equipment coverage design](EQUIPMENT-COVERAGE-DESIGN-2026-10-16.md) sections 4 to 8, [Amendment R](EQUIPMENT-COVERAGE-AMENDMENT-R-2026-10-18.md)'s static support and source-skinning contracts, and the DQ plan's stage 4 checkpoint. It retains NC2, the offline oracle model, bounded closed-host conformation, protected skin and one selected surface in every pass. The approved LBS rigid-support path remains a separate unchanged control.

## 1. Outcome and boundaries

LB-1 selects host recession beneath skinned leather. Acceptance changes from avoiding every eligible skin/leather contact to preventing skin from appearing through the actual opaque leather. Leather joins, slab junctions and popping remain requirements. Coverage does not deform the leather, repair its seams, move a protected limb, resolve equipment against equipment or change animation.

DQ1 to DQ6 remain settled. One immutable skinning profile follows a source skin through every material slice, garment binding, fit query, bounds and history. LBS remains the default. Covered DQ bodies wait for stage 4. Intentional mixed-method body/garment binding remains unsupported. Rigid sockets still use their existing joint matrices.

There are two independent version axes. The source graph selects LBS v1 or a new precise rigid-DQ v1 graph. The covering algorithm selects legacy rigid-sheet v1 or the approved posed-support v2 graph. A DQ body under a rigid plate needs the new source graph but does not require the leather algorithm. Skinned leather needs v2 under either source method. Neither silently changes the old LBS v1 output words.

The approved construction is a compound posed support set per authored host region, with finite whole-face constraints and a shared scalar recession direction. This explicitly extends v1's convex single-sheet scope. It is not sufficient to remove the rigid-part type check or skin the old sheet while retaining its cold grid and predicates.

## 2. Authored eligibility and footprint

The host map owns permission to move. A support footprint owns the area that must clear. They are distinct.

The game exports exact host source-triangle and welded position-group membership, positive strengths and zero-strength boundaries. Protected skin has strength zero. The game keeps its accepted anatomical classification as authoring policy, then explicitly reviews its geometric map. The engine does not infer eligibility from dominant bones, joint names, material, UVs, proximity or a coverage projection. A garment may use an arm influence while the arm itself stays protected, as L-3 already permits.

Each command names one disjoint positive-strength host region, one conditioned rigid coverage frame and a set of actual inward triangle ranges from bound covering sources. Source kinds are rigid or skinned. A compound command may include a plate and several straps so they constrain the same region together. They do not become overlapping independent commands. Each position group has one owner and one recession direction. Adjacent regions may share only zero-strength boundaries.

V2 stores clearance on each contributing support range, not only on the compound command. Plate and leather clearances can therefore differ. The region has an explicit total world recession limit, and each contributing source may impose a tighter displacement limit over its footprint. Every applicable limit must hold on the final host surface. Overlap uses the strictest applicable cap, never the last source or the plate's larger allowance. A plate correction that violates the leather's approved cap fails joint fit. It requires a reviewed partition, fit or cap decision rather than an automatic override. Region collar vertices also remain inside their separately authored regional displacement budget.

The footprint is the union of the current posed inward triangles projected into that command's XY plane, restricted to the authored host association. Holes remain holes. Concave boundaries remain concave. Stacked sheets contribute separate constraints, so the host must clear the innermost applicable sheet. No convex hull, outward slab thickness, global minimum plane or rest-pose proxy substitutes for this union. A crossing arm does not become a target because it shares XY with a strap.

One direction must be safe for all selected triangles in a command. Their posed projections must remain nondegenerate with consistent inward orientation and a positive, conditioned recession rate. A wraparound band therefore needs separate front, rear or upper regions where directions differ. A region boundary crossed by a strap is valid only when the unmoved boundary already clears it throughout the admitted oracle domain. Conflicting directions, an uncleared protected boundary or a folded support fail. This amendment does not introduce a general vector displacement solver.

Retain four disjoint active host commands, 32 named regions, refinement levels 1 to 4, 65,536 final host vertices/triangles, and the current 1,024 support-triangle and 256 boundary-edge limits per command. Count every component in a compound support toward those totals. Retain a hard limit of eight distinct covering sources per actor. Capacity and common-direction feasibility still require proof. Material slices of one retained source are not separate sources. No limit grows silently if the real garment needs more.

Use those same precise garment positions for support queries and for its displayed colour, depth, motion, cascade, point and outline geometry. The garment keeps its own topology and attributes. ## 4. Recession depth, shape and narrow straps

The authored 1.5 to 4 mm stance stand-off is an exterior fit requirement. It is not a runtime penetration measurement or a recession allowance. At a flat support, if skin projects outward by p metres beyond the actual inner sheet, full recession needs at least max(0, p + 1.25 C), before face, transform and slope effects. C is the authored world clearance. The quarter-clearance reserve remains driver margin, not numerical tolerance or pose slack.

A strap that already clears by 1.5 mm need not move the skin. Blindly copying a plate's 2 mm clearance would request 2.5 mm after reserve even at a flat sheet and could dent that stance. Use a separately authored leather clearance. A 0.5 mm trial value would reserve another 0.125 mm and leave 0.875 mm at the smallest stated stand-off before other losses. The owner approved investigating this trial. It is not a proved fit or engine default. The private oracle must establish the usable value against actual export precision, slopes and poses.

The maximum world recession is a separate mandatory asset value. Select it from private whole-domain requirement measurements and the owner's permitted collar appearance. Do not infer it from stand-off or inherit the plate's torso penetration allowance. Required recession includes the entire face correction. Exceeding it refuses without clamping. No sample can pass by raising the cap, moving an arm or changing a footprint.

V2 must detect a strap crossing the interior of a host triangle even when no host vertex lies beneath it. Vertex membership and v1's full-core-inside-support rule cannot do that. Fixed refinement remains source-skin-then-refine, with rational shared-edge recipes. Refinement controls the visible indentation width, not whether a finite overlap is detected.

Use whole-face constraints. The following is the exact-coordinate construction rationale, not a claim that existing binary32 helpers already implement it.

For host face F and applicable posed support triangle T, form their finite XY overlay P. Let zF and hT be their affine heights, aF the barycentric interpolation of the host's authored corner strengths, and Ccov(T) that support range's conservatively converted effective clearance. For every nonempty overlay require the final host height to be at most hT - Ccov(T). An empty overlay contributes zero.

At each overlay vertex x, compute the positive deficit zF(x) - hT(x) + Ccov(T). If it is nonpositive, no recession is required there. A positive deficit at aF(x) = 0 cannot be repaired and fails with a protected/boundary witness. Otherwise a sufficient face amplitude is the maximum of deficit/aF over the overlay vertices. Both numerator and denominator are affine. Where the denominator is positive, their ratio on a convex polygon attains its maximum at a vertex. A zero-strength boundary with a nonpositive deficit is already safe and is not divided by zero.

Reduce amplitudes over all source-ordered support triangles for that face. At each welded group, gather the maximum amplitude from its incident constrained faces and multiply by its own authored strength. Recess the original posed group by that scalar along the command direction. Every corner of a face then receives at least its strength times that face's required amplitude. Barycentric interpolation therefore supplies at least the required recession throughout each finite overlap. Gathers use immutable face results, never floating atomic scatter. Apply a command transition weight once to the complete displacement.

The reviewed numerical transcription must include the actual/nominal frame discrepancy, a positive correction rate, outward safety terms and final-float effects, extending the existing correction proof. It must refuse an unusable rounded strength or nonfinite ratio. It must not claim an exact-coordinate inequality proves the rounded final surface. The independent oracle recomputes all overlaps on the actual final words and checks clearance, equipment/host intersections, folds, caps and total world displacement. Amendment R still makes runtime construction guarded rather than fully certified.

A narrow strap can consequently move corners outside its literal footprint. Those corners must remain inside the explicitly permitted movable patch/collar. This bounded visible collar is the price of retaining fixed topology and a closed surface. Unsupported stencil clipping or per-frame cuts are not substituted. If level 4 cannot localize the collar acceptably within the cap, return the footprint, route or appearance to owner review.

Source material/shading seams retain the existing position groups and distinct shading groups. No nearest-position weld is introduced. Protected originals retain their own admitted zero-sign words. Active aliases share a canonical position. Garment joins remain authored geometry and skinning constraints. A continuous recessed body cannot close a gap between two leather pieces.

## 5. Precise DQ source graph and NC2

Use the stage-3 prepared DQ palette words from its pinned scalar CPU producer. Conversion remains once per joint, after inverseBind * jointWorld. Its largest-component W/X/Y/Z tie order, canonical signs, rigid composed-transform validation and cross-CPU input obligations remain. Keep LBS composition unchanged. Ordinary and covered DQ share uploaded input words, not a promise of identical evaluated output words.

The new precise source reference transcribes the actual stage-3 rational graph through CoverageReferenceArithmetic. Each first numerical read, operation operand and result follows inclusive F at magnitude at most 2^-126, including copied attributes. Preserve signed zeros. Validate four integer indices, nonnegative source weights, stored X/Y/Z/W order and the admitted rounded weight-total domain before dispatch.

Pin this order in the joint kernel inventory before S4.2 implementation.

1. Read and flush the four weights and all potentially copied source fields. Form the three left-associated total additions. Preserve the below-1e-8 unweighted path and the exact contributing-identity path. These paths retain source position words after required admission, while normal/tangent handling keeps the existing specified normalization behavior.
2. Start real and dual sums at positive zero. For each positive slot in X/Y/Z/W order, read its real/dual halves, form the left-associated four-product running-real dot, and negate both halves only if that dot is negative. Neither signed zero flips. Perform eight separate products and additions. Do not reorder or merge repeated indices.
3. Form four squares and the left-associated squared norm. Require finite usable arithmetic. Reprove the proposed n >= 1/8 admission lemma for this inclusive-flush graph. The ordinary proof's rounded n greater than 0.249752 is useful evidence, not a transferred NC2 proof. A failed proof blocks admission, never licenses the LBS envelope or an approximate denominator.
4. Form the unnormalized Hamilton rotation numerators with exactly the stage-3 parentheses. Compute one correctly rounded 1/n using C3's integer midpoint residual and bounded search. Apply the three-product left-associated row sums, then multiply each rotation numerator by that reciprocal. Compute each translation numerator in the pinned four-product order, multiply separately by the same reciprocal, then add it to the rotated position. No unit-quaternion shortcut or reassociation is allowed.
5. Rotate the source normal and usable tangent through the same blend without translation. Pin dot/root/three-division normalization, length > 1e-8 and source tangent squared > 1e-12 branches. Preserve tangent W, copied attributes and the absent-tangent zero convention. Division and root use the existing corrected RN32 machinery before F.
6. Validate source aliases, then run unchanged fixed refinement provenance. Reconstruct host normals/tangents from the final conformed surface in the existing ordered gathers. Garments use their own precise posed attributes and topology.

Coverage DQ and posed-support v2 each need independently derived word corpora and graph identities. Bind method/version, support algorithm/version, exact source snapshot and ordered influences, inverse binds/remaps, prepared palette input identity, recipe/map/support membership and material visibility identity into the relevant static and per-pose evidence. Keep static cache keys separate from per-frame palette identities so normal animation does not recreate assets or invalidate steady history.

Legacy constructors and JSON v1 decode as their existing LBS rigid-support contract and keep their semantic hashes. Add explicit strict v2 coverage companion/map decoding for new fields with separate hash domains. Keep attachment set JSON v1/v2 bytes unchanged. Reject unknown graph/schema versions. Old certificates cannot authorize a new method or covering graph. DQ under rigid support preserves the old conformation graph after its new source stage. Posed-support v2 deliberately has new construction outputs and cannot replace frozen v1 expectations.

NC2 remains zero tolerance for every accepted source, support-setup and final output word and for failure attribution. Retain C3 correction, B27 sign/RN32 predicates, B30 scaled crossing sides, B38 flush, C5/C6 constant component/private-array restrictions and C8 physical-module limits. DQ palette strides are eight words per joint, never reinterpreted sixteen-word matrix slots. Keep exact workers out of the physical light-filter kernel. Requalify filter certificates on posed garment and near-boundary inputs. Ambiguity queues exact work with ordered replay, never approximate acceptance or truncation.
